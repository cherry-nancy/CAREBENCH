Pay close attention to trends and critical changes in the following parameters, as they relate to general sepsis indicators:

    Respiratory: PaO2/FiO2 ratio less than 300 mmHg.

    Renal: Creatinine level greater than or equal to 2.0 mg/dL.

    Risk Synthesis & Prediction: Combine all findings, weighing the severity and progression of identified warning signs, especially those related to eSOFA criteria and infection. Based on this synthesis, determine the overall likelihood of sepsis development within the next 90 minutes.
